# A calm place to write, with your notes kept in your own GitHub account

Focus Notes is a quiet writing app for your computer. Focus mode dims everything but the lines you're writing, so the page in front of you is the only thing that asks for attention.

## Free, private, and yours

- **Free and open source (MIT).** Read every line of it in [the repository](https://github.com/prabinpebam/focus-notes).
- **No Focus Notes account or server.** The app talks only to your computer and your own GitHub account. [How it works](#how-it-works).
- **Nothing tracked.** No analytics, no cookies, no counters. [The privacy statement](privacy/).
- **Your notes are plain Markdown files,** plain text with a few symbols for formatting, that any editor can open. [Help centre](help/).

## How it works, in three steps

1. **Connect your GitHub account.** GitHub is a free website that stores files for you. Focus Notes makes a private folder of notes there for you.
2. **Write, with focus mode.** Press one key and everything but your current lines fades back.
3. **Your notes are saved on your computer and backed up to your own repositories,** so they're there on your next computer too.

## Features

- **Focus mode.** The lines you're writing stay clear and the rest of the page fades back, at your own pace.
- **A calm canvas and Spotlight.** No toolbars until you ask for them. Spotlight finds any note, command or setting from the keyboard.
- **Files and folders.** Notes are files in folders you can see, rename and move.
- **Private notes and sharing.** Private notes are sealed before they leave your computer. Share one with a passcode, and only the people you choose can read it.
- **Themes.** Light and dark themes, text sizes and fonts that suit you.

## Is it for you?

**It's for you if** you want plain files you own, a quiet editor, and don't mind a free GitHub account.

**Look elsewhere if** you need to write with others at the same time, want nothing to do with GitHub, or want databases and rich media in your notes.

## Built in the open

Every change is in [the repository](https://github.com/prabinpebam/focus-notes), with its releases and their notes. To report a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/prabinpebam/focus-notes/issues).

## For developers and AI agents

Underneath, your notes are Markdown files in a Git repository, so other tools can read and edit them too. An MCP server lets the AI agents you choose work with your notes, with your permission. The help centre has [a section for developers and AI agents](help/developers/).

## Questions

**Is it free?** Yes. Focus Notes is free and open source under the MIT license.

**Do I need to know Git?** No. Focus Notes saves and backs up your notes for you.

**Where are my notes kept?** On your computer, and backed up to repositories in your own GitHub account.

**Does it work offline?** Yes. You can write without a connection, and Focus Notes backs up when you're online again.
