Design · Components

Tables

Table editing modeled on Microsoft Loop: hover to insert, handles to move and act, drag to select, all saved as a plain GFM table.

TL;DR

Insert with /table. Hover near a row or column boundary at the table's edge to get a + insertion point. Hover a row or column to get its handle: click for a menu (insert, move, sort, align, delete), or drag to reorder. Hover the bottom or right edge for + New row and + New column. Drag across cells to select a range, then format, clear, copy, or paste over it. Loop's interaction patterns are kept. Loop features that GFM can't store (column types, widths, formulas, views) are explicit boundaries. Component IA-CMP-TABLE serves UC-TABLE-01 to UC-TABLE-08.

Inserting a table

  • /table (or "grid", "columns") from the slash menu inserts a 3 × 3 table: a header row plus two body rows, with the caret in the first header cell (UC-TABLE-01).
  • Typing a GFM header row and delimiter row (| a | b | then |---|---|) converts to a table on Enter.
  • Pasting a spreadsheet range or an HTML table on an empty line creates a table of that shape (UC-TABLE-06).

Anatomy

Frame
1 px color.border outer border with radius.small corners, and 1 px inner rules. No zebra striping (calm). Cell padding is 8 px × 12 px.
Header row
Always present (GFM requires it). color.surface fill and semibold text. It can be emptied but not deleted or moved into the body.
Column handle
A 40 × 18 px pill with three dots, centered 8 px above the hovered column. Its hit area is 32 px tall. Pressed and focused states use the accent.
Row handle
An 18 × 32 px pill with three dots, 12 px left of the hovered row, in the gutter. Hit area 32 × 32 px.
Table handle
At the top-left corner where the handles meet. Opens the table menu and selects the whole table.
+ insertion point
A 22 px accent circle with a plus, on the left edge (rows) or top edge (columns). It appears when the pointer is within 8 px of an inner boundary at the table's edge, and is drawn together with a 3 px accent line across the table at that boundary.
Edge bars
Dashed bars along the bottom edge ("+ New row", 30 px tall) and right edge ("+", 28 px wide), shown while the pointer is over the table or within 24 px below or right of it.
Cell selection
Selected cells get a color.selection fill, and the range gets a 2 px accent outline. The active cell keeps its caret.

Destructive items are last, separated by a divider, in danger color, and undoable without a dialog. Deleting the last body row leaves an empty one, because a table always has at least one.

Keyboard

The full table keymap is in Keyboard shortcuts → Tables. In short: Tab and Shift+Tab move between cells (Tab in the last cell adds a row), Enter and Shift+Enter move down and up, Mod+Enter adds a row below, Mod+Shift+↑/↓ moves a row, and Esc enters cell selection mode. In cell selection mode, Shift+arrows extend the selection, Backspace clears, and Shift+F10 (or the Menu key) opens the context menu for the selected cells, rows, or columns, so every pointer action has a keyboard path.

Copy, paste, and sort

ActionBehavior
Copy a cell rangeThe clipboard gets tab-separated text (pastes into spreadsheets), a GFM Markdown table, and HTML (pastes into documents).
Paste a range into a cellFills from that cell right and down, overwriting, and adds rows and columns if the range doesn't fit. One undo step. A toast says "Pasted 5 × 4. Table grew by 2 rows."
Paste text with line breaks into one cellThe line breaks become spaces (UNSUP-TABLE-01), with a toast the first time.
Sort A→Z or Z→A (P1)Reorders the body rows once by that column, using natural order (numbers by value, ISO dates by date, text case-insensitively). The header stays. One undo step. Nothing is remembered as a "sorted view", because Markdown keeps only the order.

Size and wide tables

  • Column widths are automatic, based on content, with text wrapping inside cells. There's no column resizing, because GFM can't save widths (UNSUP-TABLE-03).
  • A table may grow wider than the reading column, up to layout.wideContent, centered on the column so its first cells stay near the text.
  • Beyond that, it scrolls sideways inside its frame. A soft fade on the edge shows there's more, and the first column doesn't stay pinned. Arrow-key navigation scrolls the active cell into view.

With focus mode, find, and touch

  • Focus mode: the bright row is the caret's wrapped line inside its cell, spanning the canvas width (see Focus mode → Edge cases). Handles and insertion points draw above the mask.
  • Find: matches in cells are highlighted the same way, and the current match's cell scrolls into view.
  • Touch and pen: handles show for the row and column of the focused cell (no hover). Tapping a handle opens its menu, and long-pressing starts a drag. + insertion points appear on the focused cell's edges.

Accessibility

  • A native table with th header cells, so screen readers announce headers as people move between cells.
  • Handles are buttons named for what they control ("Column 2, Owner, options" or "Row 3 options"). Insertion points are named "Insert row between rows 2 and 3".
  • Structural changes are announced politely ("Row inserted below row 2", "Column moved to position 1").
  • The hover tint and insertion line are never the only signal: the menus and keyboard paths do everything they do.
