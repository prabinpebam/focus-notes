# Fix a problem

What to do when Focus Notes shows a problem, starting from its exact words.

> [!NOTE]
> **TL;DR.** The articles in this section are being written. Each will be a short page that answers one question, in plain words. Until then, use **Send feedback** (below) if you need help with any of these.

## Send feedback

Tell us about a problem or an idea from inside the app. Open it from any of these:

- **Settings:** *Send feedback*, at the bottom of the list of categories.
- **⋯ menu:** *Send feedback…*, next to *Settings…*.
- **Spotlight:** type *feedback*, *bug*, or *idea*.
- **macOS:** Help → *Send Feedback…*.

Choose *Problem* or *Idea*, give it a title, and add the details. For a problem, *Attach diagnostic report* is on: press *Preview* to see what it holds. It never includes your notes, their names, paths, emails, passwords, keys, or tokens.

- **Signed in to GitHub in the desktop app:** press *Send*. It becomes a public issue at [prabinpebam/focus-notes](https://github.com/prabinpebam/focus-notes/issues), posted as you, and the app shows its number.
- **Signed out, or in the browser:** press *Continue on GitHub*. GitHub's new-issue page opens with your title and details filled in. If you attached the report, it's on your clipboard: paste it into the issue.

If GitHub says your sign-in can't open issues there (some tokens only cover your own repositories), *Continue on GitHub* still works. *Report a problem…* in Settings → About opens the same form with *Problem* chosen.
